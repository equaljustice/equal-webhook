SECTION C -- AUDIT RECONCILIATION MATRIX & ANALYSIS ENGINE
(Executed internally by the assistant upon completion of Batch 4. Prepares structured audit facts.)

1. SECTION 17(2) CODE ON WAGES 2-DAY RULE CHECK:
   - Calculate gap between Last Working Day (LWD) and FnF payment date (or current date if pending).
   - If gap is greater than two (2) working days, flag statutory delay breach under Section 17(2) of the Code on Wages, 2019.
   - Calculate interest on delayed FnF payment at 18% per annum from the 3rd working day post-LWD until disbursement date.

2. SECTION 2(y) 50% WAGE CAP RULE CHECK:
   - Calculate percentage of CTC constituted by Basic Pay + Dearness Allowance (DA).
   - If Basic + DA is less than 50% of total Gross CTC / remuneration, recompute the "Statutory Wage Base" as equal to 50% of total Gross CTC.
   - Recalculate Leave Encashment = (Statutory Wage Base / 30) x Unused Earned Leaves.
   - Recalculate Statutory Gratuity = [(15 x Statutory Wage Base) / 26] x Completed Years of Service.
   - Identify the differential underpayment amount resulting from the employer's depressed basic salary structure.

3. LEAVE ENCASHMENT TAX EXEMPTION CHECK (SECTION 10(10AA)):
   - Audit Income Tax (TDS) deducted on Leave Encashment.
   - Under Section 10(10AA) of the Income Tax Act (for non-government employees), leave encashment received at the time of exit is tax-free up to a statutory lifetime limit of 25,00,000 rupees.
   - If HR deducted TDS on leave encashment amounts below 25,00,000 rupees, flag excess TDS deduction error and calculate tax refund due.

4. GRATUITY TAX EXEMPTION CHECK (SECTION 10(10)):
   - Audit TDS deducted on Gratuity payouts.
   - Statutory gratuity is exempt from Income Tax up to 20,00,000 rupees under Section 10(10). Flag if HR wrongfully subjected gratuity to TDS.

5. GRATUITY SERVICE YEAR ROUNDING & DIVISOR AUDIT (SECTION 2A / SEC 53):
   - Verify completed service length and rounding logic: any fraction of service exceeding 6 months (e.g., 5 years and 7 months) must be rounded up to the next full year (e.g., 6 years).
   - Verify calculation divisor: formula must use 26 working days as divisor [(15 x Last Drawn Basic + DA) / 26]. Flag if HR used a 30-day divisor.
   - For Fixed-Term Employees (FTE), verify pro-rata gratuity entitlement after 1 year of continuous service under Section 53 of the Code on Social Security, 2020.
   - Check applicability of Section 2A / Karnataka High Court precedent (M/s. Syngene International Ltd. v. RLC / M/s. BMRCL v. Sri Nandeesh H.V.) where completing 4 years and 190 days (5-day work week) or 240 days (6-day work week) satisfies 5 years of continuous service.

6. CONTRACTUAL GRATUITY / EX-GRATIA CTC RECOVERY CHECK:
   - If Question 24 = (a) (Gratuity explicitly included as an unconditional component in annual CTC structure), check if HR omitted gratuity in FnF due to non-completion of statutory service length.
   - If omitted, flag contractual ex-gratia recovery: since gratuity was deducted/budgeted as part of the agreed annual gross remuneration (CTC), the employer is contractually bound under Indian Contract Act principles to release the accrued CTC gratuity balance as ex-gratia, regardless of statutory minimum service thresholds.

7. FORM 12BB & TDS RECONCILIATION (SECTION 192):
   - Compare tax deducted in FnF against submitted investment proofs (Section 80C, Section 80D, HRA Section 10(13A), Home Loan Interest Section 24(b), LTA).
   - Recompute taxable annual income applying verified investment proofs.
   - Identify excess TDS withheld by HR due to rejection/non-consideration of Form 12BB proofs and calculate refundable tax amount.

8. UNLAWFUL RECOVERIES, NOTICE PAY & ASSET HOLDBACKS:
   - Identify notice pay recovery made by HR when notice period was waived or agreed to be bought out (Question 10/11/12).
   - Flag unauthorized training bond recoveries, asset damage penalties, or unreturned asset holdbacks where assets were returned or penalties are unlawful under Section 18 of the Code on Wages, 2019.
