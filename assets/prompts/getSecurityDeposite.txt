CONTENT BY DOCUMENT TYPE

1. Notice to Landlord

Possession Rules:
If NOT vacated / keys NOT returned (`Q7=b` or `Q7.1=b`): State that tenant retains legal constructive possession until full deposit refund with interest. Unauthorized landlord entry will be treated as trespass.

2. Police Complaint (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - BNS 2023)

Sender: Tenant to local Police Station Officer (SHO), CC Landlord.
Criminal Charges: Cite Section 318 BNS (Cheating) for taking deposit with no intent to return. Cite Section 316 BNS (Criminal Breach of Trust) if personal items withheld (`Q9=a`). Cite Section 308 BNS (Extortion) framing the landlord's refusal to return the deposit (or withholding personal belongings) as intentionally putting the tenant in fear of financial injury to dishonestly induce the tenant to forfeit their rightful money. Cite Section 314 BNS (Dishonest Misappropriation of Property) asserting that the landlord dishonestly converted the tenant’s refundable deposit money for personal use after the lease expired, satisfying the threshold for criminal misappropriation. Application to Freeze Bank Account (Section 106 BNSS, 2023) by explicitly requesting the Station House Officer (SHO) to invoke powers under Section 106 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023, to freeze the landlord’s bank account where the rent/deposit was deposited to preserve the proceeds of crime during investigation. Request for Habitual Offender Check: Ask the police to pull historical utility records, tenant verification logs, and local complaints to check if the landlord routinely engages in organized security deposit fraud against previous tenants. Mention bounced cheque (`Q12.1=a`) as clear proof of cheating.
Police Mandate: Mention Supreme Court *Lalita Kumari* precedent requiring FIR for cognizable cheating cases. Request police to inspect property, collect evidence, and check if landlord has cheated previous tenants.

3. Cheque Bouncing Notice (Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act)

Sender: Tenant directly.
Mandatory 15-Day Cure Period: Give landlord exactly 15 days from notice receipt to pay money.
Itemized Claim (STRICT RULE): List cheque amount, interest, damages, and costs in completely SEPARATE lines/paragraphs. NEVER add them together into a single total sum in the demand line (adding them together invalidates the legal notice).
